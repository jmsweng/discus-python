## Summary

The Gate 2 comparison in `GATE2_RESULTS.md` **never measured a signed correlation at 2.5 Å, and it is retracted.** Five independent defects each invalidate it on their own:

1. the diffuse data are zero by construction;
2. the total data cannot resolve 2.5 Å;
3. the extraction step forces a 3/6 result;
4. the checkerboard prediction is an open-boundary artefact;
5. the short-range prediction is statistical noise.

There was no "FFT sign-convention discrepancy".

In its place there is a pre-registered experiment (`gate2_prereg/`) designed so that averaging, sampling, boundary and form-factor confounds are removed by construction.

## Errors in the committed Gate 2 comparison

1. **The sign check is a tautology.** In `correlated_run/compare_with_engine.py` (≈ lines 95–125; `short_range_run/` has the same logic), "+dir" is the `argmax` of a line and "−dir" its `argmin`. So "+" is always ≥ 0 and "−" is always ≤ 0. Every registered prediction has equal signs at ±τ, so *any* prediction scores exactly 3/6.
   `GATE2_RESULTS.md` also misreports its own tables. It says the same peaks (+x, −y, −z) fail in both runs, but the checkerboard table shows +x, −y and +z failing.
2. **Wrong sampling line.** In unshifted `ifftn` output, zero displacement is index 0. Index 16 on the other two axes is 77.6 Å (20 Å cell) or 155.2 Å (40 Å cell) off-axis. The "peaks" sit at 4.85 Å and 38.8 Å, or at 0 Å and 29.1 Å.
3. **The window cannot resolve 2.5 Å.** The handoff's "undersampled" is the wrong diagnosis: the grid is *over*-sampled. Δh = 0.125 is in **supercell** units, which makes d*max 0.17 / 0.087 Å⁻¹ along the diagonals and d_min ≈ 5.8 / 11.6 Å. The 1D window response at 2.5 Å is +0.62 (20 Å) and +0.89 (40 Å) of the origin value. The exact windowed transform at τ = 2.5 Å along x, y and z is 93 % / 98 % of ρ(0), positive, and identical in all three directions to 10⁻⁴. Off-grid evaluation cannot rescue it.
4. **The checkerboard prediction is a boundary artefact.** c(±2.5 Å) = ±1/7 comes from open-boundary counting (112 pairs). With periodic counting (128 pairs) it is **exactly 0**. The structure is long-range ordered: c = −1 at 5 Å and +1 at 10 Å. Its x/y/z sign pattern just reflects which basis site was given which element. The basis is (0,0,0), (½,0,0), (0,½,0), (0,0,½), which is not fcc.
5. **The short-range prediction is noise.** The 2.5 Å correlations (periodic) are −0.051, +0.020 and +0.012, against SE ≈ 0.031 (≤ 1.6σ). The only clear correlation is +0.15 at 5 Å, nearly isotropic. The run also has **no `set aver,100` volume**: `sr_total.h5` is `bragg_present`, i.e. a Patterson-type quantity.
6. **The "independent Fourier comparison" compares unlike quantities.** `correlated_model.py` ≈ lines 577–627 differs from the direct prediction in three ways at once:
   - it uses the total f-density autocorrelation, not the connected covariance;
   - it is periodic where the direct sum is open-boundary;
   - it divides by an extra n_grid³.
   The committed values (6.25×10⁻⁵ everywhere vs ±0.002) follow directly from these. The JSON says `n_grid` 64 while the note says 32.
7. **Provenance.** `correlated_run/engine_comparison.json` was produced from the all-zero *diffuse* file, but the report's checkerboard table came from the *total* file. The evidence behind the table was overwritten.
8. **Tests check sanity only.** `tests/test_correlated_model.py` asserts things like finite values, \|c\| ≤ 1 and positive counts. None would catch items 1–7.

## Form factors: Waasmaier & Kirfel (1995) transcription vs DISCUS table

The paper (Acta Cryst. A51, 416–431) is paywalled, and the IUCr pages refused the connection from the build environment, so **no value was read off the PDF**. Instead, four transcriptions were compared for Cu, Ga, As and Au (`gate2_prereg/compare_form_factors.py`, output in `wk_coefficients.json`):
- ESRF DABAX `f0_WaasKirf.dat` (data supplied by the authors);
- the `xraydb` 4.5.8 table;
- ASE 3.29.0 (`ase.utils.xrdebye`, cites "Table (1)");
- DISCUS `lib_f90/element_data_mod.f90`.

| Element | f(0) DISCUS (Z) | DISCUS vs DABAX, max rel \|Δf\|, s ≤ 2 Å⁻¹ | xraydb vs DABAX | ASE vs DABAX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cu | 28.992065 (29) | 2.8×10⁻¹⁰ | identical | **1.3×10⁻¹ — ASE typo:** c = −3.774477 instead of −3.254477, so f(0) = 28.47 |
| Ga | 30.983617 (31) | 5.4×10⁻¹⁰ | identical | not tabulated |
| As | 32.988246 (33) | 3.5×10⁻⁸ | identical | not tabulated |
| Au | 78.967456 (79) | 4.3×10⁻⁸ | identical | 4.3×10⁻⁸ (matches DISCUS digits) |

DISCUS's table equals the authors' data to the last printed digit (≤ 1×10⁻⁶ absolute per coefficient). DISCUS and ASE share the same last-digit pattern, which suggests both were transcribed from the printed table. The engine therefore uses correct W–K coefficients.

This refutes the Gate 1 "untested form-factor hypothesis" as the main cause of anything at the percent level. The practically important approximation is not the coefficients but DISCUS's **nearest-neighbour s lookup at 0.001 Å⁻¹**, which is worth ~10⁻³ in intensity at s ≈ 0.5–0.9 Å⁻¹.

## Gate 1 (C1 `set aver,100`): refined diagnosis

| Model of the archived C1 volumes | total rel L2 | diffuse rel L2 |
|---|---|---|
| IT92, exact s (previous reference) | 3.888×10⁻⁴ | 1.772×10⁻² |
| W–K (DABAX), exact s | 5.8×10⁻⁵ | 1.769×10⁻² |
| W–K (DISCUS table), s rounded to 0.001, DISCUS `four_aver` semantics | 2.4×10⁻⁶ | 1.769×10⁻² |

- **Form-factor hypothesis: refuted.** Exact form factors fix the total but leave the diffuse residual unchanged.
- **Where the residual is.** At the 6924 voxels whose diffuse disagrees by > 5 %, the engine's total amplitude agrees with the model to ~0.03 (units of F). The diffuse amplitude, however, differs by ~0.9. The error is therefore in ⟨F⟩·L, which nearly cancels F there: diffuse/total is ~10⁻⁵ at those voxels, so a relative error of only 2×10⁻⁵ in ⟨F⟩·L becomes a percent-level diffuse error.
- **Leading hypothesis.** DISCUS accumulates phases as NINT(2²²·frac(step·x)) integers. For the non-dyadic step 0.01 these increments are inexact. This is untested; a rough emulation that did not reproduce the exact corner-phase decomposition (`fourier_lmn`) did not close the gap.
- **Discriminating experiment.** `gate1_c1_dyadic/` is pre-registered: the same C1 structure, almost the same window (±0.156 vs ±0.16), step 1/128 so that every phase is exact. Predictions:
  - total ≤ 1×10⁻⁹;
  - diffuse ≤ 1×10⁻⁶ relative L2;
  - plus a rerun of the archived macro that must reproduce the archived volumes to 10⁻¹².
  If the dyadic diffuse still misses at the percent level, the averaging model is incomplete and Gate 1 stays DIAGNOSED.

## Other notes

- **Friedel diagnostics on engine output are uninformative when DISCUS merges Friedel pairs.** It does so for all-odd, origin-symmetric grids (`four_test_friedel`), and the log shows "Friedel pairs merged". The Gate 2 grid has an even point count to avoid this.
- **The native `value 3DPDF` output** fits 5.517×10⁻³ × (direct transform) + constant with a 4–5 % residual on both runs. Its convention is not identified, so it is a Gate 3 item and should not be used for Gate 2.
- **This sandbox's test failures.** In this sandbox, 12 tests in `tests/test_native_3d_integration.py` fail only because `/usr/bin/time` is absent (the known false exit 127). CI has it.

## Mistakes in the handoff document (for the record)

- **C:** the problem is resolution, not undersampling. The suggested fallback of off-grid evaluation at 2.5 Å cannot work with these data.
- **E:** the committed Fourier values are not "zero" but 6.25×10⁻⁵, and the disagreement has three causes, not one divisor.
- **A:** the "Friedel and imaginary-residual diagnostics" requested are trivially satisfied on merged-Friedel engine output.
- **Framing:** the checkerboard is described as a correlated-disorder benchmark, but it is long-range ordered with zero periodic 2.5 Å correlation.
